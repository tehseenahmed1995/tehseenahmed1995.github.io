Building with AI, not just talking about it

I got genuinely excited about AI-assisted development early, and it's now part of how I actually work, not a line I add to sound current. I use it to move through spec-to-code faster, to catch things in a refactor I might otherwise miss, and to keep documentation from falling behind the code.

The judgment still has to be mine: reviewing architecture, deciding what's worth building, and owning what ships to production. But letting AI handle the repetitive parts of the day means more of my time goes to the decisions that actually need a senior engineer.
